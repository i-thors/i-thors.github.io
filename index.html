My Shorts

A small automated pipeline for one YouTube channel

My Shorts drafts, assembles, and publishes YouTube Shorts to a single channel owned by its operator. Every video is personally reviewed and approved before it goes live.

What this is

A personal, single-operator project. It is not a public service and has no other users or accounts.

Google account access

Connects to one YouTube channel to upload approved videos and read that channel's own performance statistics. See the privacy policy for the exact scopes and data handling.
