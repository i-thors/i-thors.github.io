Privacy Policy

My Shorts

Effective 1 September 2026 Contact: r1.r7.m5@gmail.com

My Shorts is a personal, single-operator automation tool that drafts, assembles, and publishes YouTube Shorts to one YouTube channel owned and controlled by its operator. This page explains what the tool accesses through Google APIs and how that access is used.

Who runs this

My Shorts is operated by an individual for their own YouTube channel. It is not a public service, it does not have other users or accounts, and it is not offered to anyone outside its operator.

What it accesses

The tool connects to the operator's own Google account through OAuth and requests exactly two scopes:

youtube.upload

Uploads pre-approved video files to the operator's own YouTube channel. Every upload is a video the operator has personally reviewed and approved before it is sent.

yt-analytics.readonly

Reads aggregate performance statistics (views, watch time, retention) for videos on the operator's own channel, used only to inform which formats to make more of.

No other Google scopes are requested, and no data is read from or written to any account other than the operator's own.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect data about viewers, subscribers, or anyone who watches the videos.
  • It does not sell, share, rent, or transfer any data to third parties, advertisers, or other services.
  • It does not use YouTube data to train any machine learning or AI model.
  • It does not access Gmail, Drive, Contacts, or any Google product beyond the two scopes listed above.

Where data lives

OAuth tokens and any analytics figures the tool retrieves are stored locally on the operator's own computer. Nothing is uploaded to a third-party server, and no external party has access to this data.

Changes to this policy

If what this tool accesses or how it uses that access changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change accordingly.
